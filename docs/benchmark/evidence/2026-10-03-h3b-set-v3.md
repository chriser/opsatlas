## The scorer defect, found after the run

Under the registered scorer, all three scope-on violations are false. Each answer was read in full:

- **sc-016** says "5 days ... the Bay 3 training room". The fact "3 onboarding days" matched on the words "3", "onboarding" and "days".
- **h-07** says "5 onboarding days ... [3]". The citation marker "[3]" supplied the "3".
- **h-04** says "Synthetic Pack E". The fact "Synthetic Pack A" matched because "a" is a stopword.

The registered scorer matches a forbidden fact by shared words. That is the generous match used to find expected facts.
The shared-word match finds everything a phrase match finds, and more. So no scope-on answer can say a forbidden fact
that this scorer missed. The same defect also inflated scope off: sc-007, sc-016, h-04 and h-07 are false violations
there too.

**The fix** (`scripts/evaluate_evidence.py`, `_said`; tested in `tests/test_evaluate_evidence_scoring.py`): a forbidden
fact is said only as a whole phrase or one of its listed aliases. Both arms were rescored from their saved answers;
nothing was re-run.
