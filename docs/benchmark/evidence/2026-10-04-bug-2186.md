# Bug #2186: an interrupted space move (design, 4 October 2026)

**How it was found.** The design red team on REF S60 found this fault (round 1's DR12, round 2's R2-4). The Human chose
the narrow fix, done first, before S60. Tier: full (state). This page is the design, for its red team on paper before
anything is built.

## What happens today

`move_document` (`services/opsatlas_sales/spaces.py`) moves a document between two spaces in separate writes, in this
order:

1. it adds the record to the target;
2. it copies the passages;
3. it moves the content history (two SQLite connections, committed one after the other);
4. it copies the images;
5. it removes the passages and the record from the origin.

A crash, or a write that fails, between step 1 and the end leaves the document in **both** spaces. Three callers then
misbehave:

- **The start-up layout** (`apply_family_layout`, called by `services/opsatlas_sales/app.py` at start). For a document
  it has not yet placed, it takes the first family space holding it as the origin and moves it again.
  - When the space it belongs in already holds it, the move raises "The target space already holds this document",
    the start-up job fails, and **the Sales workspace does not start**.
  - When the first holder is already the space it belongs in, it skips the document and marks it placed. The second
    copy then stays, unreported.
- **The layout inside a request.** `POST /api/sales/proposals` runs the layout after a claim is recorded. The same
  raise answers 500 after the claim was saved.
- **The Transfer route** (`POST /api/spaces/transfer`). It takes the first core holding the document as the origin. A
  retry answers either:
  - 409 "already in that space", when that first holder is the target; or
  - 500, from the move's `ValueError`.

  The duplicate stays, unreported. For a document the layout has already placed, nothing at start ever looks at it
  again.

Meanwhile, reads and decisions at the family level go to the first space holding the document. A space's own requests
(the `X-OpsAtlas-Space` header) reach that space's copy.

## Promises

- **B1, start-up is never blocked.** No start of the workspace fails because a document is held by two spaces,
  whatever step the interrupted move reached. Nor does a request that runs the layout fail for that reason.
- **B2, never moved automatically.**
  - The layout leaves a document that two or more spaces hold where it is, unplaced, so it is decided again once
    resolved.
  - A transfer of such a document is refused (409) with a message naming the spaces that hold it.
  - Nothing deletes, merges or rewrites either copy.
- **B3, reported.** At every start, each document held by two or more spaces of the workspace is reported in the
  start-up log, until resolved. It is reported once in the workspace's activity log, and once in each copy's own
  activity, with the same message.
- **B4, enough to choose.** For each copy, the report gives what a person needs to pick which copy stays:
  - its space;
  - its approval;
  - the SHA-256 of its record's text;
  - its number of passages;
  - its number of content-history entries (versions and activity).
- **B5, nothing else changes.**
  - A move that completes behaves exactly as today.
  - Reads and decisions on a document held by two spaces go where they go today: the first family holder at the family
    level, and a space's own copy through its header. This is stated, not changed.

**Resolving it** needs no new route. A person deletes the unwanted copy in its own space, through the existing
document delete with the space selected, which removes that copy's record, passages and content history. The next
start, or the next layout run, then decides the remaining copy as usual.

## Design

- **One holder check:** `holders(source_id)` gives every space whose own register holds the document.
  - It covers all the workspace's spaces for the transfer and the start-up scan.
  - It covers the family's spaces for the layout.
- **The layout.** For each document it would decide, if `holders` has more than one space: report it, skip it, and do
  not mark it placed. Each id is decided once, even though the family's listing returns it once per holder.
  - The move's own refusal becomes a specific error, `HeldInTwoSpaces(ValueError)`, with the same message. The layout
    catches only that error, as a backstop: report and continue.
- **The Transfer route** checks `holders` across all spaces before anything else that could pick an origin. If there is
  more than one holder, it answers 409 with the spaces and the B4 facts. The route also maps `HeldInTwoSpaces` to 409.
- **The start-up scan.** After the layout, one pass over all the spaces' registers finds the ids held more than once
  and reports them (B3, B4).
  - The activity entries are written once, keyed by the document and its holders, so a restart does not repeat them.
  - The scan only reads. Under the start-up job's lock, it writes only the activity entries.
- **The report** is one shared function, used by the layout, the route and the scan, so the message and the B4 facts
  are the same everywhere.

Not in this fix:

- completing a move automatically (rejected: it can drop an approval decision and miss half-done cases);
- making the move itself atomic or idempotent;
- a route to resolve. All three wait for REF S60's transfer, if wanted.

## The failure list

Each line is checked against B1–B5 by a named test, or given one line on why it cannot happen.

- **A crash at each step.** Crash points: after the target's record, after the passages, after the history, after the
  images, after the origin's passages.
  - At each point: the next start passes (B1), the document is reported (B3, B4), it is not moved (B2), and a retried
    transfer gets 409.
- **A write that fails at each step.** The same points, with an exception in place of a crash. The request's response
  says the transfer failed. The next start and the next request behave as for a crash.
- **A restart mid-operation:** the same as a crash. The start-up job holds the workspace's lock (S7).
- **Two requests at once:** the door gives one writer per workspace (S7). A transfer and a layout run cannot
  interleave.
- **Duplicate or stale input:** a retried transfer, or a second retry, is refused each time with the same message.
- **Access changing mid-way:** unchanged. The route's permission checks run first, as today.
- **A dependency down:** the move uses no model or service.
- **The evidence changing underneath:** a decision or an edit on either copy between the failure and a retry. The copies
  may then differ in approval or text. They are still left alone and reported, and the B4 facts show the difference.
- **Date and time, and model output:** not involved.

## Tests

- A seeded random-scenario suite for moves (`tests/scenarios.py` style). It interrupts a move at a random step, by a
  crash or a failed write, then mixes in restarts, transfers, retries, decisions and edits on either copy. After every
  step it checks:
  - B1: start-up never raises;
  - B2: no automatic move;
  - B3 and B4: reported, with the facts;
  - no document is lost: it is always in at least one space, with its text.
- Named tests for the transfer's 409 message, the layout inside a request, the start-up scan's single activity entries,
  and B5 (a completed move is unchanged).
- Guards in the guard register:
  - the holder check in the layout;
  - the route's refusal;
  - the start-up scan's report;
  - the move's specific error.

## Stated limits

- **Deleting a copy deletes its own content history.** The report says which copy holds it, so a person keeps that one,
  or knowingly loses it.
- **Until resolved,** the family-level reads and decisions see the first family holder's copy (B5), and answers may
  cite either approved copy.

## Impact map

- **Changed:**
  - `services/opsatlas_sales/spaces.py`: the holder check, `HeldInTwoSpaces`, the layout, the report;
  - `services/opsatlas_sales/routes_spaces.py`: the transfer's check and mapping;
  - `services/opsatlas_sales/app.py`: the start-up scan after the layout;
  - `services/opsatlas_sales/routes_sales_api.py`: the layout inside a request, which no longer raises.
- **New tests:**
  - `tests/test_space_transfer_interrupted.py`: the named tests;
  - `tests/test_scenarios_transfer.py`: the scenario suite;
  - entries in `tests/guard_register.py`.
- **Unchanged:**
  - the core (`src/`);
  - Tibi's engine;
  - the stores' file formats.
- **S59's checks:** no new import, store naming or private reach-in.
