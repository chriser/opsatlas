# Bug #2186: an interrupted space move (design v2, 4 October 2026)

**How it was found.** The design red team on REF S60 found this fault (round 1's DR12, round 2's R2-4). The Human chose
the narrow fix, done first, before S60. Tier: full (state, access, governance).

**Status: the stop rule applied after design round 2 (4 October 2026, night). The decision is parked for the Human.**
Nothing is built.

- **What round 2 found.** 2 Highs, 4 Mediums and 2 Lows on v2, in round 1's areas. The report is local,
  `research_notes/design-red-team-2026-10-04-bug-2186-round2.md`, with probes
  `research_notes/design-red-team-2186-probes/probe_2186r2_*.py`.
- **The flaw.** v2 put the quarantine in two core places: `Visibility` for reads, and the register's writes for
  changes. The Sales workspace reads and governs a document through paths that reach neither:
  - the family register;
  - Knowledge's records;
  - Tibi's projection and eligibility (service calls get no visibility);
  - the governance desk;
  - the content workflow's records step.

  The probes show Tibi still counting the quarantined record as eligible, and a scope decision, a spoken answer and a
  records retry going through during the freeze.
- **The options** are in `reports/Morning questions 2026-10-05.md`:
  - "absent until resolved", enforced at the register's `get` and `list`, which every path uses;
  - "stop and repair";
  - "prevent it" with a two-phase move.

The design below (v2) is kept as the record.

**v1, at `8e2ded2`.** Its design red team found 3 Highs, 2 Mediums and 5 Lows. The report is local:
`research_notes/design-red-team-2026-10-04-bug-2186.md`, with probes in `research_notes/design-red-team-2186-probes/`.
v1 kept start-up running, but its B5 ("reads and decisions go where they go today") hid two hazards that today's
failure to start avoids:

- **BR1:** a decision on one copy lands on the other copy.
- **BR2:** an internal document left in the Product Guide becomes readable by guide readers, and eligible for Tibi.

**The Human's decision (4 October 2026): quarantine.**

- While a document is held by two spaces, every copy is hidden from everyone except administrators.
- Every change is refused, except deleting one copy.
- Administrators see both copies with the facts to choose.

v2 below takes that decision and every other finding (BR3–BR10).

## What happens today

`move_document` (`services/opsatlas_sales/spaces.py`) moves a document in separate writes, in this order:

1. settle;
2. add the target's record, then update it;
3. copy the passages;
4. move the content history, on two SQLite connections committed one after the other;
5. copy the images;
6. remove the origin's passages and record;
7. delete the images no longer used.

A crash or a failed write after step 2 leaves the document in **two** spaces. Today:

- **The start-up layout** raises for a document it has not placed yet (when the target holds it), and the workspace does
  not start.
- **The proposals route** answers 500 after saving the claim.
- **A retried Transfer** answers 409 or 500.
- **A duplicate of an already-placed document** stays unreported.

Meanwhile, the two copies are served:

- **Family-level reads and the Sales decision hooks** go to the first family holder. So a decision taken on the
  playbook copy's page lands on the guide copy (BR1).
- **A space's own reads** go to its copy. So an internal document left in the guide is readable by guide readers
  (BR2).

## Promises (v2)

- **B1, start-up is never blocked.** No start of the workspace, and no request that runs the layout, fails because a
  document is held by two spaces, whatever step the interrupted move reached.
- **B2, never moved automatically.** The move itself refuses a document that another space already holds. This one
  check is inside `move_document` and covers every space of the workspace, archived ones included. The layout leaves
  such a document where it is: unplaced if it was never placed, untouched if it was. Nothing automatic rewrites either
  copy.
- **B3, quarantined while held twice.** This is the Human's decision. A document held by more than one space is
  quarantined until one holder remains.
  - **Hidden.** Every copy is hidden from everyone who does not administer a space holding it, services included, so
    Tibi too. The hiding uses the visibility layer that hides restricted documents (REF S13), so the document is left
    out of:
    - lists, views and search;
    - answers and citations;
    - Tibi's evidence and eligibility;
    - the facts map and exports.
  - **Frozen.** Every change to it is refused, except deleting one copy: approving, rejecting, publishing an edit,
    renaming, changing its details, and moving. The register is the one point where every one of those changes passes,
    so the register refuses them, with a clear message. Two writes are exempt by name, and an inventory test lists
    every write method as refused or exempt:
    - the removal of a copy;
    - settling a version committed before the quarantine.
  - **Drafts and comments** stay possible for the administrators who can see the copies. They change nothing published
    or approved, and nothing drafted can be published until the quarantine is resolved. This is stated.
- **B4, reported, with enough to choose.** To administrators only: in the quarantine report, once in each copy's
  activity, and in the start-up log at every start until resolved. For each copy, the report gives:
  - its space and its approval;
  - the SHA-256 of its record's text;
  - its passages and its content-history entries;
  - the images its text uses that are present in its space, and those present only there;
  - whether it matches the Sales record that cites it, if one does;
  - whether it is the copy the family reads today;
  - which space the document belongs in, by the layout's rule.

  The report warns when deleting a copy would lose the only image file or the only history, or would leave a Sales
  record out of step.
- **B5, nothing else changes.** A move that completes behaves exactly as today. Documents held by one space are
  unaffected. A message to anyone who cannot read a space never names that space or its copy (BR5).
- **B6, resolved by deleting a copy.**
  - An administrator deletes the unwanted copy in its own space, with the existing document delete and that space
    selected. That reaches exactly that copy (round 1 checked this) and removes its record, passages and history.
  - The quarantine lifts as soon as one holder remains: it is worked out live, from the holders.
  - A document the layout never placed is decided at its next run. A placed one stays where it is.

## Design (v2)

- **`holders(source_id)`, one function.** It gives every space of the workspace whose register holds the document,
  archived spaces included (their partitions are read from disk). The move, the quarantine, the scan and the report all
  use it.
- **The quarantine list.**
  - It lives in `spaces.json`, which the Spaces store owns: `quarantined: {source_id: {holders, since}}`. It is written
    under the workspace's lock in three places:
    - by the start-up scan;
    - by a move that fails after its target's add, best-effort in the failing request;
    - by the layout's backstop.
  - A document is quarantined when it is on the list and `holders` still names more than one space. That check is
    live, so a resolved document is free at once. The next start's scan drops entries that no longer hold.
  - A document duplicated again later re-enters the list, with a new report (BR7).
- **Hiding.**
  - The core's `Visibility` takes an optional provider of the documents hidden from everyone but space administrators.
    The access layer passes `app.state.quarantined`, which the Sales app sets; it defaults to none, so a lone core is
    unchanged.
  - `can_read` and the fast path take the provider into account. Administration is worked out whenever restrictions or
    quarantined documents exist.
  - Services never administer.
- **Freezing.**
  - The register takes an optional predicate, `frozen(source_id) -> reason | None`, which the Sales app sets for every
    core. The register's writes, except the exempt ones, refuse a frozen document with a `DocumentFrozen(ValueError)`
    error carrying the reason.
  - The routes map it as they map `ValueError` today: 409 in the Sales routes, and `ContentError` → 409 in the content
    routes through the workflow's own refusal.
- **The move.** `move_document` takes the workspace's `holders` and refuses with `HeldInTwoSpaces(ValueError)` when
  another space holds the document. The Transfer route and the layout pass `holders`.
  - The layout catches only `HeldInTwoSpaces`: it reports, quarantines and goes on.
  - The route answers 409. The route's permission checks come first, and its message names only the spaces the
    requester can read.
- **The start-up scan.** After the layout, one pass over every space's register finds the ids held more than once, puts
  them on the list, and reports them.
  - It is best-effort: a failure to report is logged and never fails the start (BR6).
  - The activity entries are written once per list entry.
- **The report** is one public function in `spaces.py`, used by the scan, the layout, the move's failure path and the
  route.
  - It reads each copy's facts through the stores' own methods. It names `content.db` nowhere outside `spaces.py`
    (S59).
  - A part it cannot read is shown as unreadable, never as zero.

Not in this fix:

- completing a move automatically (rejected by the Human);
- making the move atomic, idempotent or two-phase (a later transfer redesign);
- a route to resolve (the existing delete resolves).

## The failure list

- **A crash at each step.** Crash points:
  - after the settle;
  - after the target's add;
  - after its update;
  - after the passages;
  - between the history's two commits (the history is then in both spaces);
  - after the history;
  - after the images;
  - after the origin's passages;
  - after the origin's record, which leaves orphaned images.

  At each point, the next start passes (B1). A document held twice is quarantined: hidden, frozen and reported (B3,
  B4). A retried transfer gets 409 (B2). No document is lost.
- **A write that fails at each step:** the same points, with an exception. The failing request records the quarantine,
  best-effort, then reports the failure. The next start behaves as after a crash.
- **A failed write in the start-up layout or the scan:** the start continues. The report is best-effort and logged
  (B1).
- **A restart mid-operation:** as a crash. The start-up job holds the workspace's lock (S7).
- **Two requests at once:** one writer per workspace, at the door (S7).
- **Duplicate or stale input:** retries are refused each time with the same message.
- **Access changing mid-way:** the quarantine hides from everyone but administrators, whatever their grants. Grants are
  unchanged by it.
- **A dependency down:** the move uses no model or service.
- **The evidence changing underneath:**
  - Between the failure and the quarantine's first record, a decision or an edit on either copy is possible in the
    window before the next start or retry. The report shows the difference.
  - After the quarantine is recorded, changes are refused.
- **Date and time, and model output:** not involved.

## Tests

- **A seeded random-scenario suite** for moves (`tests/scenarios.py` style):
  - it interrupts a move at a random step, by a crash or a failed write;
  - it mixes in restarts, transfers, retries, decisions and edits on either copy, reads as a reader, an administrator
    and Tibi, and a **resolution step** (deleting a chosen copy).
  - After every step it checks:
    - B1, start-up never raises;
    - B2, no automatic move;
    - B3, hidden from non-administrators and Tibi, and changes refused;
    - B4, reported with the facts;
    - B6, the quarantine lifts at resolution;
    - no document lost.
- **Named tests:**
  - the transfer's 409 and its message for a requester who cannot read the other space;
  - the layout inside a request;
  - the scan's single activity entries;
  - the inventory of the register's writes, refused or exempt;
  - B5, a completed move unchanged;
  - a lone core, with no quarantine provider, unchanged.
- **Guards:**
  - the move's holder check;
  - the layout's skip;
  - the visibility provider;
  - the register's freeze;
  - the scan's report.

## Stated limits

- **Drafts and comments** on a quarantined copy stay possible for administrators (B3).
- **The window before the quarantine is recorded.** A change in the window between the failure and the next start (or
  the failing request's own best-effort record) is possible. The report shows it.
- **Deleting a copy deletes its own history and images.** The report says which copy holds them, and warns.
