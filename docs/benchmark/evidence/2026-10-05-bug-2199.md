# Bug #2199: Tibi Knowledge serves a restricted family document's text (design, 5 October 2026)

**How it was found.** The independent review of Bugs #2191 and #2192 (5 October 2026, night) found it beside the route
#2192 removed. Tier: full (access, REF S13). This page is the design, for its red team on paper before anything is
built.

## What happens today

The Tibi Knowledge page's routes (`/api/tibi/...`, `services/opsatlas_sales/tibi_api.py`) are workspace routes. They
are served by the Sales app itself, not a space's core, and read the OpsAtlas family's three spaces through the family
register and Knowledge.

- **The visibility they miss.** The request's visibility, set by the access layer, is the Product Guide's. It is not
  applied to these routes' family reads, and it does not cover the other two family spaces.
- **The reads, guarded only by permission** (`tibi.knowledge.read`, or `governance.read`):
  - `GET /sources/{identifier}`: a document's title and full text;
  - `GET /knowledge`: Knowledge's catalogue rows, their text included;
  - `GET /spoken`: the spoken-answer variants;
  - `GET /ontology`: the product facts;
  - `GET /governance/answers`, `/governance/agenda`, `/governance/statements`: the governance desk's findings.
- **The decisions,** guarded by `tibi.knowledge.approve`, `tibi.spoken.approve` or `governance.findings.resolve`:
  - `POST /knowledge/{id}/review`, `/knowledge/{id}/resolve`;
  - `POST /spoken/{id}/review`;
  - `POST /ontology/{id}/confirm`;
  - `POST /governance/answers/{id}/review`.
- **What the review showed.** A product owner refused a restricted Product Guide document on the governed content route
  (404) is served its full text by `GET /sources/{id}` (200).
- **The Sales API already does this right for Tibi's conversations.** `readable()` in `routes_sales_api.py` builds, for
  the conversation's owner, the family spaces they may read and one `Visibility` per space, and keeps only the rows they
  may read. `project_facts()` drops the product facts that rest on a record they may not use.

## Promises

- **K1, one rule.** What a person may read of the family's documents is decided by one function. The Sales API's
  `readable()` and the Tibi Knowledge routes both use it, for the conversation's owner and the signed-in person
  respectively. It covers:
  - the family spaces they may read (`documents.read`);
  - the documents and folders restricted from them in each space (REF S13);
  - conversation-style records, which always apply, as today.
- **K2, the reads.** Every Tibi Knowledge read route returns only what the person may read:
  - `GET /sources/{id}` answers 404 for a document they may not read, the same answer as for one that does not exist;
  - `GET /knowledge` and `GET /spoken` leave out the records and variants resting on a document they may not read;
  - `GET /ontology` drops the facts resting on such a record, as the Sales API's `project_facts()` does;
  - the governance desk's three read routes leave out the findings on such records.
- **K3, the decisions.** A decision on a record or variant resting on a document the person may not read answers 404,
  with nothing changed. Administrators of the space read everything, so for them nothing changes.
- **K4, nothing else changes.**
  - A person who may read everything (an administrator, or a reader of an unrestricted family) sees exactly what they
    see today.
  - The Sales API's answers to Tibi are unchanged: the same function, the same inputs.
  - Tibi's engine is unchanged.

## Design

- **`family_reader(app, person_id) -> keep(row_or_source_id) -> bool`,** in `services/opsatlas_sales/routes_sales_api.py`
  or a small module beside it. It holds `readable()`'s known-owner branch as it is today.
  - `readable()` calls it for the owner. The other branches stay: an unknown conversation sees the Product Guide's open
    documents, and Tibi itself sees the whole family.
  - The Tibi Knowledge routes call it for the request's signed-in person.
- **In `tibi_api.py`,** each read route filters through `keep`. The decision routes look up the record's source and
  answer 404 unless `keep` allows it.
- **The product facts** use the same projection as the Sales API (`project_facts`), shared rather than copied.
- **The digest the page shows** (`GET /knowledge`) is computed over the rows the person sees, as the Sales API's
  `caller_digest()` does.

## The failure list

- **Access changing mid-way:** a restriction added, or a role removed, between requests. Each request builds `keep`
  afresh, so the next request sees it. Within one request, `keep` is built once, and the page's rows are read after it.
- **Two requests at once, a crash, a restart, a failed write:** the reads write nothing. The decisions keep today's
  writes once `keep` allows them, and refuse before writing when it does not.
- **Duplicate or stale input:** a decision on a record now hidden from the person answers 404.
- **The evidence changing underneath:** a document restricted after the page loaded. The next read or decision sees
  it.
- **A dependency down, date and time, model output:** not involved.

## Tests

- **Per route,** with a document restricted to the administrator in each family space it can live in (the guide and the
  playbook), a product owner outside its audience, and the administrator:
  - each read leaves it out, or answers 404;
  - each decision answers 404 with nothing changed;
  - the administrator sees and decides as today.
- **The review's failing test**
  (`research_notes/independent-review-tests/test_review_2192_family_text_routes.py`) is adopted into the suite.
- **The leak sweep** (`tests/test_leak_sweep.py`) is extended to the Tibi Knowledge routes, with the planted marker in a
  restricted family document.
- **A guard:** `family_reader` switched off (keeping everything) makes the route tests fail.
- **K4:** the Sales API's existing tests for `readable()`, unchanged, and a test that an administrator's view of each
  route is unchanged.

## Stated limits

- The Product Guide's open documents stay readable by everyone signed in, as today.
- Tibi itself, with no conversation named, still sees the whole family, as today (REF S10, S11).

## Impact map

- **Changed:**
  - `services/opsatlas_sales/tibi_api.py`: the read and decision routes;
  - `services/opsatlas_sales/routes_sales_api.py`: `readable()` uses `family_reader`; `project_facts` is shared.
- **New:**
  - `tests/test_tibi_knowledge_visibility.py`;
  - the adopted review test;
  - leak-sweep cases;
  - a guard.
- **Unchanged:**
  - the core;
  - Tibi's engine;
  - the stores.
- **S59's checks:** no new import across a boundary.
