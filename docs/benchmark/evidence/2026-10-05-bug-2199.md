# Bug #2199: Tibi Knowledge serves a restricted family document's text (design v2, 5 October 2026)

**How it was found.** The independent review of Bugs #2191 and #2192 (5 October 2026, night) found it. Tier: full
(access, REF S13).

**Status: the stop rule applied after design round 2 (5 October 2026, night). The decision is parked for the Human.**
Nothing is built.

- **What round 2 found.** 3 Mediums and 4 Lows on v2, in round 1's areas. The report is local,
  `research_notes/design-red-team-2026-10-05-bug-2199-round2.md`.
- **The flaw.** v2 decides from the documents the registers hold now. But the page shows copies that outlive their
  documents: records, overlaps, spoken variants, facts and governance answers. And the policy it must honour is
  IAM's restriction rows, which survive a deletion and do not follow a move. The frontend also drops the refusal's
  reason.
- **The options** are in `reports/Morning questions 2026-10-05.md`:
  - decide from IAM alone: everyone inside every family restriction;
  - administrators of all three family spaces only.

  Either comes after the IAM fault found beside it, Bug #2202.

v2 below is kept as the record.

**v1, at `0f3e272`.** It filtered each route's rows through the Sales API's `readable()` rule. Its design red team
found 4 Highs, 7 Mediums and 6 Lows. The report is local: `research_notes/design-red-team-2026-10-05-bug-2199.md`,
with probes in `research_notes/design-red-team-2199-probes/`. A per-row filter cannot keep hidden content out:

- **Rows that pass the filter carry hidden records.** A claim row includes the topic record it overlaps, with its full
  text (DB1).
- **Governance items name hidden documents by title, with no source id** to filter on (DB2).
- **The shared rule's exemption leaks.** Its exemption for conversation-style records lets a product owner read
  System-space text (DB3).
- **Three of the five decisions change several records** (DB4).
- **The other shapes leak too:** spoken variants, the facts export, and a digest that rebuilds the shared graph (DB5–DB7).

**v2 takes the simpler rule S13 already uses for derived views** (`derived_guard`, `src/assistant/api/access.py`):

- A view built from many documents is not shown at all to a person from whom some of those documents are hidden.
- They get 403, with the reason.

The Tibi Knowledge page is such a view: built from every record of the OpsAtlas family, and every document those records
rest on. Tibi's own channel, which the Sales API serves, has the same kind of gap. That is a bigger, separate fix, so it
is its own Bug, #2201 (DB11).

## What happens today

The Tibi Knowledge routes (`/api/tibi/...`, `services/opsatlas_sales/tibi_api.py`) are workspace routes. They serve the
family's three spaces through Knowledge, the governance desk and the family register, guarded by permission only.

- **Reads:**
  - `GET /knowledge`, which the Talk with Tibi page also reads for its topics;
  - `GET /sources/{id}`;
  - `GET /spoken`;
  - `GET /ontology`;
  - `GET /governance/answers`, `/governance/agenda`, `/governance/statements`.
- **Decisions and runs:**
  - `POST /knowledge/{id}/review`, `/knowledge/{id}/resolve`;
  - `POST /spoken/{id}/review`;
  - `POST /ontology/{id}/confirm`;
  - `POST /governance/answers/{id}/review`;
  - `POST /governance/statements/run`.
- **The rest:** `ws-ticket`, `status` and `machine` serve no family content.

## Promises (v2)

- **K1, one rule, one place.** A dependency, `family_guard`, is put on every Tibi Knowledge route: all the reads,
  decisions and runs listed above, and none of the other three. It answers 403 `RESTRICTED_EVIDENCE`, "Built from
  documents you may not read; ask a space owner", to a person who either:
  - may not read one of the family's three spaces (no `documents.read` there); or
  - has any document of the family's three spaces hidden from them by a restriction (REF S13).

  It counts every document, approved or not, because the page shows records resting on documents in any state.
- **K2, nothing partial.** A person who passes the guard sees the routes exactly as today. A person who does not sees
  none of them, and their decisions are refused before anything is read or written.
- **K3, nothing else changes.**
  - An administrator, and anyone who may read the whole family, sees and decides exactly as today. Today that is
    everyone who uses the workspace.
  - The Sales API and Tibi's channel are unchanged (Bug #2201).
  - Tibi's engine is unchanged.
- **K4, an inventory.** A test walks the Tibi router and requires the guard on every route, except a named list of
  three (`ws-ticket`, `status`, `machine`).

## Design

- **`services/opsatlas_sales/family_access.py`** (new) holds
  `hides_any_family_document(app, person_id) -> bool`. For each family space it checks two things:
  - `documents.read` (the policy);
  - any document of that space's register hidden from the person (`Visibility(...)` for the person in that space,
    `hides_any`).

  It is the same per-space construction `readable()` uses for a conversation's owner, without the exemption for
  conversation-style records (DB3).
- **`family_guard(request)`,** in `tibi_api.py`, applies it to the request's signed-in person. A request with no person
  is refused (401), as the routes' permission checks already do.
- **The routes** get the guard as a dependency, next to their permission.
- **The frontend:** the Tibi Knowledge page and the Talk with Tibi page's topic list show the reason when they are
  refused. This is checked in the browser; if a page breaks on a 403, it gets a small fix to show the message.

## The failure list

- **Access changing mid-way:**
  - A restriction added or lifted, or a role removed, between requests. The guard runs per request.
  - Within one request, a restriction landing between the guard and a decision's write (`/api/iam` passes the door) is
    a moment's window, stated.
- **Two requests at once, a crash, a restart, a failed write:** the guard reads only. Decisions keep today's writes once
  allowed.
- **Duplicate or stale input:** a decision by a person now refused answers 403.
- **The evidence changing underneath:** a document moving between family spaces mid-request (DB17). The guard reads each
  space once, and a document moving into a space the person may not read is counted there.
- **A dependency down, date and time, model output:** not involved.

## Tests

- **Per route, for three people:**
  - a product owner with a guide document restricted from them;
  - one with a playbook document restricted;
  - one with no role in the playbook.

  Each read and decision answers 403 with the reason, and the decisions change nothing.
- **The administrator** sees each route exactly as today (K3).
- **The inventory test (K4)**, and the review's own failing test, adopted into the suite.
- **The leak sweep** (`tests/test_leak_sweep.py`) gets a family case (DB9):
  - a marker in a restricted document of a family space;
  - people with roles in the family spaces;
  - `{identifier}` routes filled with real ids.
- **A guard in the register:** `family_guard` switched off makes the route tests fail.

## Stated limits

- **A person with anything hidden in the family loses the whole Tibi Knowledge page,** not just the hidden part. That is
  the derived-views rule (S13), chosen because a partial view leaks (v1's round). Today nobody but the Human uses the
  workspace.
- **Tibi's channel** is Bug #2201's.
- **A moment's window** between the guard and a decision's write.
