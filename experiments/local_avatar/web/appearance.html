How this first model works

Apple Vision supplies facial landmarks. Similarity alignment keeps the eyes in a consistent position. Training frames alone define 48 compact appearance components. Our randomly initialized 31,024-parameter neural network learns to map 64 landmark coordinates to those components. A linear predictor uses the same data and decoder for comparison.

353 training frames, 73 validation frames and 54 test frames come from separated sections of one synthetic recording. The final test is locked; it was not used to choose model weights. This is not a test on a different recording or camera.

Training took about six seconds on Metal. Warm CPU patch prediction for both predictors took 0.38 ms at p95; portrait compositing took 3.23 ms at 560 × 700. Those measurements exclude tracking, video encoding and audio processing. The checkpoint is about 7.4 MB.
