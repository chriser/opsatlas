What remains before better training

These sections come from one short synthetic recording and were previously used for the appearance experiment. They are not an independent session test. The neural model changes less than the tracked target; low jitter alone would give a misleading impression of quality.

We need a separate camera recording with relaxed closed lips, clear mouth shapes and pauses, followed by a fresh held-out session. Six geometric mouth components are learned here; all twelve rig controls and perceived phoneme alignment remain unvalidated.
