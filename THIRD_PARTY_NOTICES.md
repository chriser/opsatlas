## The Spirit (Talk with Tibi stage animation)

`frontend/src/tibi/Spirit.tsx` adapts the particle motion of "The Spirit" by Edan Kwan
(https://github.com/edankwan/The-Spirit): positions kept in a float texture, pulled towards a moving point and
pushed along curl noise. Its simplex-noise-with-derivatives shader is from Ashima Arts / Stefan Gustavson's
webgl-noise (MIT).
